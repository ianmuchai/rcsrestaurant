# Homepage Posters

Two original RC's promotional assets replace the four photo-and-copy blocks.
The homepage retains its video hero, floating controls, menu carousel and footer.
Marketing paragraphs and the hero tagline were removed; navigation, prices,
legal information and payment messages were not shortened.

## Assets

- `public/images/rcs-posters/classics-v1.webp`: 1536 x 1024, 165,402 bytes.
- `public/images/rcs-posters/coffee-v1.webp`: 1536 x 1024, 148,982 bytes.

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool, not the API/CLI fallback. Each poster
was generated separately with the existing RC's logo and relevant food image as
references. The PNG outputs were encoded to WebP at quality 90 without resizing
or changing the composition. Original PNGs remain in the image-generation folder.

These are reference-guided promotional recreations, not unedited product photos.
Neither poster makes a price, discount or availability claim. CJ's supplied the
layout inspiration, not artwork, branding or advertising text.
